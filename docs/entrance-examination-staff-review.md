## Confirmed responsibilities

1. The Registrar records the application and checks the applicant and guardian relationship through the school's intake procedure. Email verification proves control of the recorded mailbox; it does not prove legal guardianship.
2. Finance configures the exam fee and records any verified payment or authorized waiver. Keep LRD and USD separate. A free examination must have an explicit zero fee.
3. Authorized staff prepare questions, answer keys and available points. The Principal or School Administrator reviews and approves the question set before an online examination is scheduled. Candidates receive the approved questions without answer keys.
4. Staff assign the applicant, check eligibility and arrange the venue, invigilator and examination window. Scheduling uses Liberia time (GMT). Send the access code within the 24 hours before start and more than five minutes ahead. An issued code expires 15 minutes after start or 24 hours after issue, whichever comes first.
5. The guardian verifies the recorded email and receives a separate candidate examination code. Codes must be kept private. A candidate can sign in only during the permitted window and start or resume one examination attempt.
6. The candidate checks that answers show Saved. Automatic saving follows a pause in typing; manual Save remains available. Explicit submission closes editing. At the deadline, the server submits answers it has already received. Unsaved local changes cannot be added afterward. The invigilator records any interruption for school review; the system does not silently grant extra time.
7. Authorized staff mark every submitted answer and provide appropriate feedback. A different Principal or School Administrator reviews and approves the marks. The marker cannot self-approve. Separate accounts must represent actual separate reviewers during live school work.
8. A leader explicitly releases the approved result to the current verified guardian email. The email contains a private result code, not the marks. The code expires after seven days; replacement codes invalidate previous codes. The private page shows the result and feedback without an admission decision.
9. To correct approved marks, a leader records a reason and reviews withdrawal. The original marks and release remain in history, and old result codes stop working. Revised marks need separate approval and a new explicit release to the verified guardian.
10. Admission remains a separate school decision. This module does not automatically score answers, set pass/fail rules or admit/reject an applicant.
