# Redeemer payroll rules for school approval

Redeemer's International Academy

**Status:** Draft for school approval. **Prepared:** 7 November 2026. **Policy version:** To complete.

HR and Finance should complete the decisions below, attach the authoritative references, and obtain the school's approval. The existing manual payroll workflow remains available. This draft does not authorize automated deductions, payments or a production release. Do not include employee salaries, bank details, tax identifiers or passwords in this shared approval copy.
