## About the school — draft wording

Welcome to Redeemer's International Academy in Paynesville, Liberia. Our motto, “Academic and Moral Excellence,” expresses the standard we aim to uphold in learning, conduct and school life.

This website helps families explore the school, ask questions, request a visit and begin an admission application. The school office can explain current admission requirements and the next steps for your family.

Before publication: confirm the establishment year, school history, approved education stages/grades, registration/accreditation wording and any claims about facilities or achievements. Do not claim Ministry approval or publish an identifier unless the school verifies it.

## Parent handbook — proposed structure

This is an outline for a school-authored handbook, not an adopted policy:

1. Welcome, purpose, version date and approval authority.
2. School contacts, hours and calendar.
3. Arrival, attendance, late arrival, absence and collection arrangements.
4. Learning, homework, assessment and reporting.
5. Conduct, behaviour review and school-family concerns process.
6. Learner wellbeing, safeguarding and the school's named reporting contact.
7. Health, illness, medication, allergies and emergency arrangements.
8. Uniform, supplies, meals and transport/services actually offered.
9. Fees, approved payment channels and financial queries.
10. Privacy, photographs, school records and portal use.
11. Visits, events and communication expectations.
12. Acknowledgment and review date.

Every policy section requires school review before release. Do not invent disciplinary consequences, health procedures, legal wording or charges. The public page may say “The approved parent handbook will be published here” until the school approves a version.
